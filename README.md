## 4. hand avoid game    

--

#### rules  
1.use your hand to move the player (circle)  
2.if the opponent (red square) hits the player, it's game over  
3.the opponent will get faster and faster  
4.survive as long as you can  
5.enjoy the game ;)

## 5.a shooting game

--

### rules
1.use your hand to move the player (circle)  
2.if the opponent hits the player, it's game over  
3.there are many types of opponents  
4.there are items in the game. The smal shapes are the ones.  
5.If you close your hand, you can shoot and destroy opponents.(there is a bug here so I'm trying to fix it)  
6.the opponent will get faster and faster  
7.survive as long as you can  
8.enjoy the game ;)  (well you can't shoot because of the bug though)
